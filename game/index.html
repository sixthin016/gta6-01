How to play

WASD move / drive · mouse drag orbit camera · Shift sprint / boost · E enter or steal a car · Click shoot · Space jump / handbrake · R reload · M mission list · P pause.

Stealing cars and fights raise your NBPD wanted stars. At 3 stars the police set roadblocks; at 4 the helicopter joins. Stay out of sight to cool down.

Park a car inside the Harbor hideout to keep it in your garage. Finish missions to recruit Dune, Mara and Kip — each changes how the city treats you.

Hurricane Solana is coming. Finish the 16-mission story before it lands.

NEON BAY

Sixteen missions. Five stars. One hurricane.

Rae Vega came back to Neon Bay with nothing but a fast car and a faster reputation. The crew is waiting, the NBPD is watching, and Hurricane Solana is 48 hours out. Build the crew. Take the score. Get out before the storm does.

Original game — city, characters, vehicles and story are all invented for Neon Bay. No network needed; everything runs from these files.
